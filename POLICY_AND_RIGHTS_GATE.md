# Mandatory policy, accuracy and rights gate

A single failed or uncertain item means HOLD. Do not publish.

## Historical integrity
- [ ] Every material factual claim has a traceable source in the source register.
- [ ] Conflicting estimates are attributed and qualified; no false precision.
- [ ] Quotations are exact, contextual, and sourced; otherwise remove them.
- [ ] Reconstructed scenes do not invent identifiable real-person dialogue or imply unseen events as fact.
- [ ] Dates, maps, borders, uniforms, weapons, vehicles, insignia, weather and geography have been checked.
- [ ] Separate contemporary evidence from later interpretation.

## Footage and asset rights
- [ ] Every video clip, image, map, sound, font, music cue and graphic has a recorded creator/source, exact licence, permitted commercial use, attribution requirement and proof URL/file.
- [ ] Licence permits monetized YouTube use, editing, worldwide distribution and the intended duration.
- [ ] No watermarked, scraped, pirated, unlicensed or unclear-rights material.
- [ ] AI-generated assets comply with provider terms for commercial use and retain evidence of the applicable terms.
- [ ] Archival status/public-domain claim verified for the exact item and jurisdiction; age alone is not sufficient.
- [ ] No reused/duplicated footage or repetitive stock filler.

## YouTube and audience safety
- [ ] Community Guidelines and advertiser-friendly content reviewed.
- [ ] No glorification, praise, recruitment, or instructional support for violent extremist actors.
- [ ] Graphic violence is contextual, necessary, and non-gratuitous.
- [ ] Realistic synthetic/altered content disclosure is applied when required by YouTube.
- [ ] Metadata and thumbnail accurately represent the documentary; no fabricated claims or deceptive packaging.
- [ ] Privacy, defamation, and applicable legal risks reviewed.

## Editorial and technical quality
- [ ] Narration is original, natural, intelligible and fully synchronized.
- [ ] Scenes are actual moving footage, not still-photo slideshows.
- [ ] Each scene has a unique visual purpose; no duplicated shots or repeated narration.
- [ ] Sound mix, captions, spelling, dates, maps, transitions and end screen checked.
- [ ] Final export plays from beginning to end; 16:9, 1080p minimum where source quality permits, MP4/H.264 or a YouTube-compatible format.
- [ ] Thumbnail readable on mobile and truthful.
- [ ] Final title, description, sources/credits and synthetic-content declarations reviewed.
