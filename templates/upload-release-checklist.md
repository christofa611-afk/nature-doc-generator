# Untapped History & Storytelling — upload release checklist

Complete this for every long-form documentary and Short. A checklist is not evidence of completion: attach the Studio screenshot or record the verified setting and date.

## A. Editorial and policy gate
- [ ] Original script with a distinct thesis, research notes and source register.
- [ ] Claims, dates, quotations, maps, uniforms, weapons and scene details checked.
- [ ] Fact, interpretation and disputed evidence clearly distinguished.
- [ ] Every asset has recorded origin, licence/permission and commercial-use scope.
- [ ] No unlicensed footage, music, sound effects, maps, fonts, logos or third-party artwork.
- [ ] No copied scripts, scenes, thumbnails, or superficial template repetition.
- [ ] Realistic synthetic/altered scenes disclosed in YouTube Studio when required.
- [ ] Paid-promotion declaration selected only when the upload contains a covered paid placement, sponsorship, endorsement or other compensated promotion. Ordinary YouTube-served ads do not trigger this setting.
- [ ] Violence, hate, extremist content, misinformation and advertiser-suitability reviewed against current policy.
- [ ] Full final master watched/listened to; captions, narration and render checked.

## B. Search and packaging
- [ ] One primary search intent supported by current keyword evidence where available.
- [ ] Three truthful title candidates reviewed; final title clearly matches the film.
- [ ] Thumbnail is legible at mobile size and accurately represents the story.
- [ ] Primary phrase appears naturally in title and first two description lines.
- [ ] Unique description includes synopsis, verified chapters, sources, relevant playlist and next-video link.
- [ ] Tags are relevant spelling/context variants only; no keyword stuffing.
- [ ] Chapters start at 00:00, contain at least three valid ascending timestamps, and match the final master.
- [ ] Correct category, language, audience setting and subtitles selected.
- [ ] Playlist selected; public playlist link added only after it exists.

## C. End screen — long-form
- [ ] Final 15–20 seconds intentionally composed with clear visual space.
- [ ] Relevant next video or focused playlist selected.
- [ ] Subscribe element selected.
- [ ] Elements do not obscure important action or captions.
- [ ] Previewed on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Saved in YouTube Studio and re-opened to verify.
- [ ] Destination, verification date and screenshot/reference recorded.

## D. Cards — long-form where eligible
- [ ] At least one genuinely relevant video or playlist card added at a natural transition.
- [ ] No card interrupts the cold open, critical evidence or emotional climax.
- [ ] Card destination exists and is accessible.
- [ ] Previewed, saved and re-opened to verify.
- [ ] Timestamp and destination recorded.
- [ ] If the video is Made for Kids, note cards/end screens are unavailable; do not misclassify audience to enable them.

## E. Short-specific bridge
- [ ] Short tells a complete, valuable micro-story; it is not a truncated long-video excerpt.
- [ ] First second presents a clear visual/question; no long logo intro.
- [ ] Captions are readable and accurate; mobile-safe composition.
- [ ] Related long-form video is linked using YouTube's available related-video feature when eligible.
- [ ] No assumption that Shorts views or watch time count toward the long-form public watch-hour goal.

## F. Release and measurement
- [ ] Upload privately/unlisted for final QA; public release only after all gates pass.
- [ ] Record URL, publish date/time, final title/thumbnail, primary keyword, playlist and end-screen/card destinations.
- [ ] Capture 24-hour, 7-day, 28-day and 90-day metrics where available.
- [ ] Record views, eligible public long-form watch hours, average view duration, average percentage viewed, impressions, CTR by source, first-30-second retention, end-screen click rate and card performance.
- [ ] Review results monthly against the preceding comparable period; note sample size and traffic-source mix.
- [ ] Keep total views, Shorts engaged views and eligible public long-form watch hours separate.

## Stop conditions
Do not publish if any factual, rights, policy, audience-setting, disclosure, or technical question remains unresolved. No target or schedule overrides this gate.
