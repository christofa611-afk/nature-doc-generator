# Winter War geographic map source audit

**Review date:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** source leads identified; map production remains HOLD pending rights and cartographic review.

## 1. Treaty border / ceded territory — strongest primary-source lead

### Presidential Library of Russia: original treaty maps
- Record: “Maps to the Peace Treaty between the USSR and Finland”
- Date: 12 March 1940
- Catalogue identifies the item as an original, held at the Archive of Foreign Policy of the Russian Federation; digitised copy supplied through the Federal Archival Agency.
- URL: https://www.prlib.ru/item/1317215
- Research value: strongest located lead for the actual treaty-annex map, rather than a modern simplified redraw.
- Rights status: **NOT CLEARED**. Archival availability does not itself grant reproduction or derivative-use rights. Confirm the library's terms, source image conditions, and any archive restrictions before copying/tracing.

### Treaty text / territorial wording
- FRUS 1940, Document 287: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1940v01/d287
- Article II describes the areas transferred to the USSR and explicitly refers to an attached map. The US diplomatic report also notes that its own rough newspaper map was not an exact boundary description.
- Use this record to cross-check named areas and treaty wording; do not digitise a border from the FRUS text alone.

### Definitive boundary protocol context
- FRUS 1941, Document 38: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1941v01/d38
- Reports completion and ratification of the definitive boundary protocol and maps in May 1941. This is useful to explain why the treaty text alone is not a precision geospatial dataset.
- The boundary protocol map itself still needs a separately identified, rights-reviewed copy.

## 2. Pre-war Karelian terrain and historical place geography

### National Land Survey of Finland — Karelian Maps
- URL: https://www.maanmittauslaitos.fi/en/e-services/karelian-maps
- Official NLS Finland service describes pre-1939 maps of the Karelian Isthmus and Ladoga Karelia prepared by the National Land Survey and Finnish military Topographic Service.
- Includes topographic and parish maps at 1:20,000 and smaller scales, depicting settlements, roads and terrain before the Winter War.
- The NLS page says old maps can be downloaded free; **free download is not the same as an explicit commercial derivative-use licence**. Obtain and archive the exact terms for the selected sheet.
- Appropriate use: verify historical place names, pre-war roads and terrain. Do not present a pre-war topographic sheet as the 1940 treaty boundary.

## 3. Suomussalmi / Raate Road operations

### U.S. Army Combat Studies Institute — *Wrath of Achilles: Essays on Command in Battle*
- PDF: https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/combat-studies-institute/csi-books/WrathOfAchilles_EssaysOnCommandInBattle.pdf
- Search result identifies a map titled “Map 5. Suomussalmi: 30 November – 8 December 1939” with roads, Finnish defensive line, Soviet axes and dated locations.
- Research value: useful secondary map lead for checking the Suomussalmi sector and place relationships.
- Rights status: **NOT CLEARED**. Do not screenshot, trace or reuse the published map until Army University Press / US Government rights status and any third-party material are checked. Prefer to use it as a research reference and draw a fresh schematic only from independently corroborated facts.

### University of Maryland repository — operational overview map
- PDF: https://api.drum.lib.umd.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/5c5a5802-e5a9-4d53-8931-7386c1e47440/content
- The document labels an overview map of the Soviet initial attack (30 November–16 December 1939), describing major axes of advance.
- Research value: contextual cross-check only; not a source for detailed Raate Road unit movements.
- Rights status: **NOT CLEARED**. Check dissertation/repository terms and underlying map provenance before reuse or tracing.

## 4. Additional archive map leads

### Harry S. Truman Library — “Map of the Soviet Army in Finland”
- URL: https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/maps/m648-map-soviet-army-finland
- Catalogue says the library is unaware of copyright claims but explicitly places rights assessment and attribution responsibility on the user (“use at your own risk”).
- **Not cleared for publication.** Its catalogue warning is not a positive commercial-use licence. Could be a research comparison only.

### Russian Presidential Library — pre-war Finnish front map
- URL: https://www.prlib.ru/item/1324335
- Catalogue describes an original 1:200,000 map of the Finnish front dated 12 October 1939, held in the Russian State Archive of Socio-Political History.
- **Not cleared for publication.** Verify rights, scale, projection and exact geographic coverage.

## 5. Production decisions

1. **Map A — 1939 strategic overview:** build only after selecting a base dataset with an explicit commercial-use licence. Use NLS Finland historical sheets for pre-war terrain verification; cross-check the international border and place labels separately. Keep it schematic if no licensed historic boundary layer is available.
2. **Map B — Suomussalmi / Raate Road:** do not collapse the 163rd Division's Suomussalmi fighting and the 44th Division's Raate Road fighting into one continuous pocket or imply that every Soviet soldier was captured. Use the Army University Press and UMD maps as research references only until rights and the operational sequence are verified against the existing Raate source notes.
3. **Map C — 1940 territorial settlement:** use the treaty-annex map at the Russian Presidential Library as the primary cartographic research reference, with FRUS 1940 Document 287 for Article II. Obtain permission/licence before reproducing or tracing the archival image. If permission is unavailable, produce a text-only named-area graphic or a separately sourced schematic with a prominent not-to-scale label.
4. No source image, boundary, route arrow or troop symbol is approved for the final documentary by this audit.
5. Every delivered map must carry a source note, date, scale/disclaimer, licence evidence and second-person accuracy sign-off.

## Final decision

**HOLD — no geographically precise Winter War map is approved yet.** The most promising leads are now identified, but archive access, copyright, exact boundary geometry and independent cartographic verification remain outstanding.
