# Winter War narration — pronunciation and human listening checklist

Status: reviewer aid only. Pronunciations below are practical guides, not a substitute for a Finnish speaker's review.

## Names and terms to listen for

| Term | Suggested guide | Review note |
|---|---|---|
| Suomussalmi | SOO-oh-moo-sahl-mee | Finnish place name; preserve the separate vowel sounds. |
| Raate | RAA-teh | Finnish long first vowel; do not pronounce like English “rate”. |
| Motti | MOT-tee | Double consonant; keep the short, clear Finnish rhythm. |
| Karelian Isthmus | kuh-REE-lee-an ISS-muhs | English-language geographical term. |
| Mannerheim | MAH-nehr-hime | Finnish surname; ask a Finnish speaker to confirm the chosen English rendering. |
| Leningrad | LEN-in-grad | Historical name used for the city in 1939–1940. |
| St Petersburg | saint PEET-erz-burg | Modern name; use only in the explanatory parenthetical. |
| Moscow Peace Treaty | MOS-koh PEES TREE-tee | Keep treaty name clear and unhurried. |

## Full-track listening pass

Listen to the complete WAV and mark each issue with a timestamp:
- [ ] Incorrect or inconsistent pronunciation.
- [ ] Misread date, number, abbreviation or place name.
- [ ] Clipped consonant, swallowed word or unnatural pause.
- [ ] Misplaced emphasis that changes the meaning.
- [ ] Audible artefact, metallic timbre, breath glitch or sudden change in voice.
- [ ] Repeated or missing words compared with the approved script.
- [ ] Sentence pacing too fast for comprehension.
- [ ] Abrupt joins between generated chunks.
- [ ] Volume or tone changes between sections.

## Required reviewer output

For every issue record:
1. Timestamp in the WAV.
2. Exact script wording.
3. What the voice actually says or how it sounds.
4. Corrected wording or pronunciation instruction.
5. Whether the affected audio needs regeneration.

Do not mark this review complete based on a short sample. The entire 8:55 track must be heard. If the narration changes, regenerate and re-check the full track and its caption timings.
