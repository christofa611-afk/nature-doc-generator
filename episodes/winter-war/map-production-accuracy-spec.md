# Winter War maps — production and accuracy specification

Status: required map assets not yet produced; no territorial map is approved.

## Existing graphics: permitted scope

The three generated motion graphics currently in the repository are:
- `motti_explainer.mp4`: explicitly schematic operational explanation, not a map of the Raate Road.
- `winter_war_timeline.mp4`: date sequence, not a geographic map.
- `peace_treaty_timing.mp4`: treaty signature and ceasefire distinction, not a territorial map.

Do not use any of these as evidence for exact geography, front-line positions, troop routes, or ceded borders.

## Required geographic map assets

### Map A — 1939 strategic overview
Show Finland, the Soviet Union, Leningrad (present-day St Petersburg), the Karelian Isthmus, and the pre-war international border. Label the map as a simplified overview. Use a traceable boundary dataset or authoritative historical map; retain the dataset licence and source record.

### Map B — Suomussalmi / Raate Road operations
Show the named locations and route relationships only after checking against a historical operational map. Distinguish the Suomussalmi fighting involving the 163rd Division from the Raate Road fighting involving the 44th Division. Do not draw an uninterrupted encirclement that implies no Soviet breakout. Include a clear legend and a non-scale disclaimer if the geometry is schematic.

### Map C — Moscow Peace Treaty territorial settlement
Use Article II and its attached map/protocol as the controlling primary source for the territorial provisions. Verify the border line against a second reputable historical cartographic source. Clearly distinguish:
- territory ceded by Finland;
- the Hanko lease arrangement;
- the pre-war border;
- the post-treaty border.

Do not invent a precise line from a prose summary. If a suitable authoritative boundary cannot be verified, use a text-only list of named areas rather than a misleading map.

## Design and evidence rules

- All maps must be original editorial graphics, not screenshots or traced commercial maps.
- Keep the underlying source URL, date accessed, dataset version, licence, and any transformation notes with the asset.
- Do not use modern national boundaries as if they were 1940 borders.
- Label modern place names parenthetically where helpful, without replacing historical names.
- Every front line, arrow, boundary and troop movement must be supported by a cited source.
- Maps are explanatory graphics; they do not replace authentic moving historical footage.
- No map is approved for publication until a second reviewer checks geography, dates, labels, legend, and source rights.
