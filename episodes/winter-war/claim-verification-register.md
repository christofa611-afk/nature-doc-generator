## Source record used in this review

1. **US Department of State, FRUS 1940, Document 287** — contemporary diplomatic summary of treaty terms and protocol.  
   https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1940v01/d287
2. **US Department of State, FRUS 1940, Document 288** — contemporary report of the Finnish government's public explanation of the peace decision.  
   https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1940v01/d288
3. **Via Karelia, “Battles in Suomussalmi 30 November 1939 – 7 January 1940”** — battle chronology and Raate Road overview.  
   https://viakarelia.fi/en/themes/military-history/winter-war-30-november-1939-13-march-1940/battles-in-suomussalmi-30-november-1939-7-january-1940/
4. **Oleg Bozko, “The Ukrainian 44 Division in the Finnish Winter War, 1939–1940,” Journal of Military History** — specialist study of the 44th Division; particularly important for avoiding the false claim that every element was destroyed.  
   https://www.sshs.fi/content/uploads/2023/02/Sotahistoriallinen-aikakauskirja-26-2007.pdf
5. **Pasi Tuunainen, “Motti Tactics in Finnish Military Historiography since World War II,” International Bibliography of Military History 33 (2013)** — historiographical discussion of motti tactics and their logistical/operational context.  
   https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Pasi-Tuunainen/publication/273550370_Motti_Tactics_in_Finnish_Military_Historiography_since_World_War_II/links/59dc655c458515e9ab4b561a/Motti_Tactics_in_Finnish_Military_Historiography_since_World_War_II.pdf
6. **US Marine Corps, Finland Study** — secondary operational overview of the February offensive, breakthrough and territorial settlement.  
   https://www.marines.mil/portals/1/Publications/Finland%20Study_1.pdf
7. **National WWII Museum, “White Death”** — secondary overview of Soviet preparation and the February offensive.  
   https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/white-death
8. **Mannerheim.fi, “The Winter War”** — Finnish historical overview of the invasion, defensive line and Soviet breakthrough.  
   https://mannerheim.fi/10_ylip/e_talvis.htm
9. **Treaty of Moscow, 12 March 1940, including protocol** — treaty text reproduced in the American Journal of International Law.  
   https://documentsdedroitinternational.fr/ressources/TdP/1940-03-12-TraitedeMoscou%28Finlande%29%28enanglais%29.pdf
