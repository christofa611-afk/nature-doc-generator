# Winter War operational chronology — verification notes

Status: research notes for editorial review; not narration approval.

## Suomussalmi and Raate Road

- 30 November 1939: the Soviet 163rd Rifle Division began its advance toward Suomussalmi. Finnish forces in the immediate area were initially very limited.
- 11 December 1939: Finnish forces under Hjalmar Siilasvuo launched a counterattack and cut the Soviet supply route along the Raate direction.
- 28 December 1939: Finnish forces retook the Suomussalmi village area after the 163rd Division withdrew; the division was no longer an effective offensive force in that sector.
- 1–6 January 1940: the decisive fighting against the Soviet 44th Rifle Division on the Raate Road took place.
- 7–10 January 1940: a core of the 44th Division, including its command, escaped the encirclement. Do not state that every soldier or the entire division was annihilated.

## Editorial cautions

- Treat Suomussalmi and Raate Road as connected but distinct phases of fighting.
- Avoid exact casualty totals unless independently checked against competing Finnish, Soviet and Ukrainian records.
- Explain motti as a method of isolating and dividing forces, not a guaranteed formula or a synonym for annihilation.
- Do not portray the Finnish victory as effortless; the operation depended on reinforcements, supply preparation, parallel winter routes and costly fighting.
- Distinguish the 163rd Division's withdrawal from the 44th Division's later breakout.
