## Required additions before narration lock
1. Treaty territorial provisions and a map from a traceable authoritative source.
2. Detailed operational chronology for Suomussalmi, Raate Road and the February breakthrough.
3. A careful account of pre-war negotiations and the Mainila incident, if included.
4. Reliable, clearly defined casualty and displacement estimates, if included at all.
5. Contemporary Finnish and Soviet accounts where they materially differ.

## Narration claims intentionally excluded
- Exact Soviet casualty totals.
- Exact Finnish casualty totals.
- Exact displaced-person totals.
- A definitive attribution of the Mainila incident.
- Claims that the Soviet Army was uniformly incompetent or never adapted.
- Claims that Finland won or defeated the Soviet Union.
