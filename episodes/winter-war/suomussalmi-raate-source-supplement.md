# Winter War — source supplement and chronology cross-check

Status: research supplement; fact-check approval remains HOLD.

## Cross-checked operational sequence

| Date | Event | Editorial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Nov 1939 | Soviet 163rd Rifle Division advances toward Suomussalmi from the Juntusranta and Raate directions. | The Via Karelia chronology reports the opening advance and the limited Finnish forces initially present. |
| 7 Dec 1939 | Finnish forces abandon the burned Suomussalmi village area during the opening withdrawals. | Avoid saying the village was continuously held by Finland from the invasion onward. |
| 11 Dec 1939 | Siilasvuo's forces begin a counterattack and cut the Soviet supply connection in the Raate direction. | This began a sequence of fighting, not an instant Finnish victory. |
| 27–29 Dec 1939 | Finnish forces attack the 163rd Division; Soviet troops withdraw from Suomussalmi on 28 Dec and the remaining positions are cleared the following day. | The exact end date varies by account; narrate the withdrawal on 28 Dec and qualify the mopping-up. |
| 1–6 Jan 1940 | Main fighting against the 44th Division on the Raate Road. | Bozko's study dates the decisive battles to 1–6 January. |
| 7 Jan 1940 | Finnish accounts mark the victory at Raate Road; most of the division's equipment was captured or abandoned. | Avoid absolute claims that every soldier or the whole division was destroyed. |
| 7–10 Jan 1940 | A core of combat-capable personnel, including the divisional command, broke out of the encirclement. | Explicitly retain this qualification, supported by Bozko's study. |

## Motti: important explanatory nuance

Motti operations relied on more than cutting a road. The Raate Road case involved Finnish movement away from the main road, including a prepared winter route across frozen lakes, supply arrangements, ski mobility, blocking positions and coordinated flank attacks. Explain this as a context-dependent operational method, not a universal trick.

## Required narration/map adjustments

- The current narration's broad description of the Suomussalmi and Raate Road battles is acceptable as a short summary, but add the 11 December counterattack and the distinction between the 163rd and 44th Divisions if expanding this section.
- Do not use a map that depicts the 44th Division as fully annihilated or a complete encirclement with no breakout.
- Any troop strengths, casualty totals, equipment totals or exact route distances require a separate evidence check before narration or graphics use.
- Keep all source media out of the production unless its individual reuse rights are verified. These links are research sources, not blanket media licences.
