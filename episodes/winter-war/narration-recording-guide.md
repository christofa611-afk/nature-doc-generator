# Narration recording and edit preparation

## Voice direction
- Language: English, internationally intelligible pronunciation.
- Delivery: calm, authoritative, measured documentary narration; not theatrical, not sensational.
- Pace target: approximately 130–140 words per minute.
- Use short natural pauses after dates, turning points and important qualifications.
- Preserve qualifications such as "some", "in this sector", "by March", and "Finland remained independent"; do not simplify them away.
- No fabricated character dialogue, quotations, or acted Soviet/Finnish speeches.
- Avoid exaggerated accents or imitations of identifiable people.

## Pronunciation guide
- Motti: commonly approximated "MOT-tee".
- Suomussalmi: "SWO-moo-SAL-mee" (check native Finnish pronunciation).
- Raate: "RAH-teh".
- Karelian: "kuh-REE-lee-uhn" (English pronunciation).
- Mannerheim: "MAN-er-hime" (English pronunciation; native Finnish differs).
- Leningrad: "LEN-in-grad".
- Helsinki: "HEL-sin-kee".

## Recording requirements
- Record the complete approved narration in one consistent voice.
- Capture at 48 kHz where supported; use clean, uncompressed WAV if the TTS provider permits.
- No music under key dates, treaty details or nuanced historical claims.
- Use restrained natural ambience only where licensed or original.
- Remove clipping, clicks, long dead air and robotic overemphasis.
- Do not time captions until the final narration and final picture edit are locked.

## Edit structure
Use the 17 unique scene IDs in scenes.json. Each scene should have a distinct visual purpose and actual continuous motion. Do not reuse the same clip for multiple scenes merely by cropping, mirroring or changing speed. Maps are original explanatory graphics, not substitutes for moving footage in every scene.

## Quality control
- Listen to the full narration while reading the approved script.
- Check every date, name, place and military term.
- Confirm all scene transitions match the narration's meaning.
- Ensure no AI visual depicts a disputed or invented event as documentary fact.
- Export captions only from the final master.
