# Thumbnail art direction — Winter War

## Visual concept
A single Finnish soldier in winter camouflage in the right foreground, tense and alert, with a Soviet armoured column and infantry softened by snow haze in the left background. Snow-laden conifers frame the scene. The composition should communicate vulnerability, scale and winter conditions rather than a triumphant victory.

## Text
Large: 105 DAYS
Small: FINLAND UNDER ATTACK

Keep the copy short, high contrast and readable at mobile size. Leave clear negative space for typography. Do not add fabricated unit insignia, flags, medals, maps or historical quotations.
