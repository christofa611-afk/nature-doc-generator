# Moving-footage generation prompts — Winter War

**Use:** production prompts for short, distinct video clips. These are not evidence that a clip has been generated. Every AI output remains rights-status HOLD until the provider's applicable commercial terms are verified and the actual file is reviewed.

## Global visual lock
Photorealistic live-action historical reconstruction; Finland, winter 1939–1940; cold natural light; restrained documentary camera language; realistic human motion and physical weight; historically plausible clothing and equipment; no modern objects, no contemporary flags or insignia unless checked, no text, no subtitles, no logos, no gore, no heroic fantasy, no slow-motion montage clichés. Natural movement must remain visible throughout the clip. Avoid frozen subjects, morphing faces, extra limbs, changing weapons, unstable vehicles, impossible snow physics and repeated compositions. Do not imitate a specific living filmmaker or copy a copyrighted film scene.

## Scene prompts

### S01 — The invasion begins
Wide documentary tracking shot of a Soviet infantry column and period military trucks moving across a snow-covered Finnish border road at dawn, late November 1939. Soldiers march with natural uneven rhythm, truck exhaust drifts sideways in the cold, boots compress snow, branches sway in the wind. Camera tracks parallel at walking speed, no staged combat. Keep uniforms and vehicles consistent within the shot.

### S02 — Geography
Create an original editorial map, not a video-generation clip. Show Finland, the Soviet border, Leningrad and the Karelian Isthmus with restrained labels. Animate a slow camera push from Baltic-region overview to the Isthmus. All borders and labels must be checked against a reliable 1939 map before final use.

### S03 — Multiple-front advance
Low moving camera follows a long column of period Soviet trucks and marching infantry on a narrow snowbound forest road in Finland, winter 1939. Tires compress slush, exhaust and breath are visible, soldiers move naturally, the forest closes around the road. Documentary realism, no explosions or invented battle action.

### S04 — Finnish mobilisation
Interior-to-exterior documentary shot in a Finnish rural mobilisation area, 1939. Finnish reservists in period winter clothing check skis, fasten boots, shoulder packs and move out in small groups. Close camera observes real hand movements and fabric texture; no readable invented documents, no modern gear.

### S05 — Terrain
Continuous aerial-style glide above a vast Finnish winter landscape of frozen lakes, dense conifer forest and narrow roads. Wind visibly moves treetops and sweeps loose snow across the lake surface. Camera advances smoothly at a plausible speed; no artificial map overlays.

### S06 — Suomussalmi / Raate Road context
Ground-level tracking shot along a narrow snow-covered forest road near Suomussalmi in winter 1939. Period Soviet vehicles are visible at a distance, partially obscured by trees and snow haze; small Finnish ski patrol silhouettes move through the tree line without firing. Do not stage a specific unverified moment or claim the shot is archival.

### S07 — Motti explanation
Create an original top-down operational map. A long road is shown through forest; animated blocking points divide a column into separated pockets. Add simple arrows to show how supply and mutual support are disrupted. Label this as a simplified explanatory diagram, not a scale map of a specific engagement.

### S08 — Human cost
Restrained documentary reconstruction: Finnish field medics and soldiers carefully move a wounded comrade on a sled through snowy woodland. Natural coordinated walking, sled runners cut into snow, breath visible. No blood, no graphic injury, no identifiable real person.

### S09 — Mannerheim Line
Slow lateral tracking shot revealing dispersed Finnish fieldworks on the Karelian Isthmus: snow-covered trenches, timber structures, obstacles and occasional modest concrete positions integrated into the landscape. Avoid depicting a continuous monumental concrete wall. No readable signage.

### S10 — Soviet command adapts
Inside a dim field headquarters, Soviet staff officers in period uniforms study a large paper map, point to sectors, move markers and exchange notes. Camera slowly tracks across the table; hands and papers move naturally. No fabricated legible orders, insignia or identifiable historical leaders.

### S11 — Artillery concentration
Wide documentary view of Soviet artillery crews preparing and firing period field guns in winter 1940. Crew members load, step back and brace; recoil, smoke and snow movement should be physically plausible. Camera remains at a safe distant angle. No graphic casualties.

### S12 — Breakthrough
A moving ground-level shot of period Soviet infantry advancing behind tanks through a damaged winter defensive sector in February 1940. Soldiers maintain plausible spacing and movement; smoke drifts across the field; camera tracks sideways behind cover. Do not show invented close-up violence or anachronistic equipment.

### S13 — Finnish leadership faces negotiations
Exterior documentary reconstruction of generic Finnish officials in winter coats entering a government building in Helsinki, March 1940. Camera follows from behind; snow and wind move naturally. Do not generate recognizable likenesses or claim a specific person is shown.

### S14 — Treaty
Close documentary shot of a hand turning pages of period diplomatic papers on a wooden table, winter daylight across the paper, fountain pen beside the documents. Camera makes a slow controlled push-in. Do not generate readable fake treaty wording, signatures or official seals.

### S15 — Displacement
Respectful wide shot of Finnish civilian families carrying practical belongings along a snow-covered road during winter relocation in 1940. Adults and children walk at a natural pace; camera follows from a respectful distance. Avoid melodrama, identifiable real people or invented dialogue.

### S16 — Legacy
Present-day documentary footage of a quiet Finnish forest and memorial landscape, explicitly captioned as modern footage if used. Wind moves trees and grass; camera makes a slow steady forward movement. No staged reenactment.

### S17 — Closing landscape
A continuous forward glide through a snow-laden Finnish forest at dusk, opening onto a wide quiet landscape. Snow falls gently and tree branches move in the wind. End with a clean visual area for a separately created, fact-checked postwar map.

## Clip acceptance test
Reject a clip if motion is absent or merely a zoom across a still; if a person, weapon, vehicle, uniform or landscape morphs; if it contains modern or anachronistic details; if it repeats another scene; if it depicts an unverified specific event as fact; or if commercial rights are unclear.
