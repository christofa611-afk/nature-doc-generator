## Mandatory acceptance gate

A scene can move from HOLD to ACCEPTED only when every applicable gate passes.

### A. Genuine motion
- [ ] The clip contains continuous, observable subject or environmental movement.
- [ ] It is not a still image with a pan, zoom, parallax effect or particle overlay.
- [ ] Movement remains plausible from first frame to last.
- [ ] The clip has sufficient usable duration and resolution for the edit.

### B. Historical and visual integrity
- [ ] Uniforms, headgear, weapons, vehicles, tools and architecture are plausible for the stated place and date.
- [ ] No modern objects, anachronistic insignia, incorrect flags or impossible geography appear.
- [ ] Faces, hands, limbs, weapons and vehicles remain stable; no morphing, duplication or sudden object changes.
- [ ] Weather, snow, smoke, recoil, walking, vehicle movement and camera motion obey plausible physics.
- [ ] The scene does not imply a specific documented event unless that event and visual details are independently verified.
- [ ] No invented readable documents, maps, treaty text, insignia or quotations appear.

### C. Editorial quality and originality
- [ ] The composition is distinct from every other scene in the episode.
- [ ] It advances the narration at the exact point where it is used.
- [ ] It is not generic stock filler or a repeated visual with superficial changes.
- [ ] It does not rely on a slideshow, frozen portrait, static AI image or looping background.
- [ ] It contains no graphic detail beyond the episode's editorial needs.

### D. Commercial rights and policy
- [ ] Provider and exact tool/model/version are recorded.
- [ ] Terms applicable on the generation/export date are saved.
- [ ] Commercial use and monetized YouTube publication are explicitly permitted.
- [ ] Any attribution, watermark, disclosure or other conditions are recorded and satisfied.
- [ ] The input prompt and source/reference assets are owned, licensed or otherwise lawfully usable.
- [ ] No copyrighted character, film recreation, protected music, logo or unlicensed third-party asset is present.
- [ ] Synthetic/altered-content disclosure decision is documented for YouTube.
- [ ] No misleading historical depiction, fabricated evidence or deceptive packaging is used.

If a rights term is ambiguous, the clip remains HOLD. Do not infer permission from a free tier, watermark-free export, public availability or the historical age of the subject.
