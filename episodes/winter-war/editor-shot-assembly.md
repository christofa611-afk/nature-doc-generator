## Assembly rules
- No still-image slideshow sections.
- No generic stock footage that could be mistaken for a specific historical event.
- Do not reuse clips to pad runtime.
- Use maps only when the narration needs geography or operational explanation.
- Sound effects must be original or licensed for monetized use.
- Keep all asset licence evidence in the episode folder or linked asset register.
